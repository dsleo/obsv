## Design Context

### Users
Primary users are readers and analysts following the French presidential election. They need to understand what happened, which groups may be affected, what evidence supports that analysis, and how reported polls evolve.

### Brand Personality
Credible, modern, futurist, and editorial: a serious newspaper experience that treats evidence and uncertainty carefully.

### Aesthetic Direction
Newspaper-inspired hierarchy with warm paper tones, refined display typography, restrained orange accents, strong headlines, and a clear chronological news feed. Keep analysis visually distinct from reported facts.

### Design Principles
1. Lead with the news: show the poll trend and the event chronology before secondary analysis.
2. Evidence before inference: show dates, source links, freshness, method and uncertainty.
3. Distinguish official poll observations from synthetic or modelled hypotheses.
4. Use an editorial composition, clear hierarchy and readable French copy; avoid dashboard boilerplate.
5. Precompute analyses before publication; never make a reader wait on a model request.

# Radar 2027: election evidence and executable pipeline

For the current product claim and release boundary, start with the [Radar README](./README.md). The [method note](./method.md) gives a shorter explanation of this pipeline.

Updated 16 October 2026. This document describes the `/radar` path. Synthetic Nemotron personas are a demographic/filtering frame; they are not observed voters or an opinion poll. The selected complete-commune frame described below supersedes the original regional pilot for group exploration.

## Election collection

The ingestion spine is data.gouv.fr's [harmonized election collection](https://www.data.gouv.fr/datasets/donnees-des-elections-agregees): [general results](https://data-pipeline-open.s3.sbg.io.cloud.ovh.net/elections/general_results.parquet) and [candidate/list results](https://data-pipeline-open.s3.sbg.io.cloud.ovh.net/elections/candidats_results.parquet), joined on `id_election` and `id_brut_miom`. The two tables carry registered counts, abstentions, blank/null ballots, expressed votes and actual candidates/lists by polling station. We aggregate to `code_commune`, the finest location supported by Nemotron. Official [general](https://static.data.gouv.fr/resources/donnees-des-elections-agregees/20260204-102727/schema-general-results.json) and [candidate](https://static.data.gouv.fr/resources/donnees-des-elections-agregees/20260204-102742/schema-candidats-results.json) schemas describe the fields. The candidate schema has inconsistent capitalization for `nuance`; the importer uses the actual Parquet column.

Preserve the actual option in each election. A party-family crosswalk is a separate, dated and reviewable table. In particular, [official municipal nuances cover only certain larger communes](https://www.conseil-etat.fr/site/actualites/elections-municipales-2026-le-conseil-d-etat-rejette-les-recours-de-lfi-et-de-l-udr-contre-la-circulaire-attribuant-des-nuances-politiques-pour-l); unlabelled local lists remain `unknown/local`, not an invented national party. [2026 municipal rules changed](https://www.interieur.gouv.fr/actualites/communiques-de-presse/elections-municipales-et-communautaires-2026-bilan-chiffre-du-premier-et-du-second-tour) for small communes and Paris/Lyon/Marseille. Round-2 availability is selective. Never map a persona to a polling station without an address; [INSEE notes bureau identifiers are not fully stable](https://www.data.gouv.fr/datasets/bureaux-de-vote-et-adresses-de-leurs-electeurs).

The Parquet importer balances `abstentions + blancs + nuls + exprimés = inscrits` at each bureau. For a single-choice ballot it also requires `sum(candidate votes) = exprimés`. A cell failing that second check remains in the file for turnout analysis but is marked `voteVectorEligible: false`; its candidate tallies are preserved separately and the calibration CLI refuses to form a candidate probability vector from it. This matters especially for 2020 small-commune municipal ballots, where one elector could vote for several individual candidates. The importer writes `qa-summary.json` and source-file SHA-256 digests. Sixteen rounds were imported locally: the five core rounds plus 2017 presidential T1/T2, 2019 European, 2020 municipal T1/T2, 2021 departmental T1/T2, 2021 regional T1/T2, 2024 legislative T2 and 2026 municipal T2. In the core import: 35,245 communes for each 2022 presidential round; 35,228 for 2024 European; 35,232 for 2024 legislative T1 with four vote-vector-ineligible communes; 34,884 for 2026 municipal T1 with 46 vote-vector-ineligible communes. The 2020 municipal T1 import retains 34,917 communes, of which 24,928 are turnout-only due to multi-choice ballots or candidate-total mismatches. These counts are extraction diagnostics, not a claim of full national population coverage. A spot-check against the [Ministry's published Eu (76255) result](https://www.resultats-elections.interieur.gouv.fr/municipales2026/ensemble_geographique/28/76/76255/) matched 5,363 registered, 1,865 abstentions, 82 blank/null, 3,416 expressed and all three list vote counts exactly.

## Persona ontology and geography

`fr-electorate-4` uses a fixed tag-ID enum. Direct Nemotron fields: age, sex, education, broad occupation and household. Deterministic derived tags: age/education/occupation/household groups. [INSEE COG 2026](https://www.insee.fr/fr/information/8740222) resolves commune **plus department** to an official code, and the [INSEE 2025 density grid in 2026 commune geography](https://www.insee.fr/fr/information/8571524) supplies dense/intermediate/rural and 2023 municipal population. The registry includes the under-1,000-resident threshold and Paris/Lyon/Marseille municipality tag. Unresolved or ambiguous names stay unknown. Historical election joins require a date-appropriate COG crosswalk where a commune changed.

Specific professions and former professions are tagged only on an explicit phrase in `professional_persona`, retaining the evidence phrase and `narrative_rule` provenance. They are excluded from default quantitative filters until reviewed. `scripts/radar/export-tag-audit.ts` exports a deterministic sample of candidate matches and their text; `scripts/radar/apply-tag-audit.ts` applies dated reviewer decisions to individual rows, records a SHA-256 digest of those decisions, and promotes only approved matches to `audited_narrative`. This is a row-level audit mechanism, not proof that the extraction rule generalizes to every persona. A held-out error-rate audit is still needed before broad use. “Retired former university professors in rural communes” requires three independent tags; “profession libérale” is never inferred from broad self-employment. Housing tenure, income, vehicle ownership, citizenship, registration and vote history are not in Nemotron. Absence of a tag means unknown, not false.

The LLM can return only enum tag IDs. The deterministic compiler applies `all` (intersection), `any` (union within that clause), and `none` (exclusion), then checks conflicts, observed counts, geography coverage and unaudited-tag dependence. Negative tags require an observed closed field; Paris/Lyon/Marseille absence is decidable only after an exact commune resolution. The model separates missing **membership** attributes, which make a group unfilterable, from outcome or exposure **measurement** needs. Named communes are resolved via COG rather than packed into the LLM enum.

## Vote-vector estimator

1. Audit Nemotron's demographic fit before changing weights. Where necessary, calibrate adult population weights against dated INSEE marginals, checking concentrated weights and effective support. A synthetic million rows is not a human sample of a million voters.
2. For each election, estimate eligibility and registration propensity from external human/admin evidence. Scale the resulting expected registered masses to official `inscrits` by supported geography. Presidential and European/municipal electorates have [different eligibility rules](https://www.service-public.fr/particuliers/vosdroits/F1367). These masses cannot be derived from Nemotron names or biographies.
3. Estimate heterogeneous individual priors `p_iek` from human survey microdata with age, sex, education, PCS and geography, preferably a hierarchical multinomial model. [CEVIPOF ENEF 2024](https://www.sciencespo.fr/cevipof/fr/actualites/science-ouverte-l-enquete-electorale-2024-du-cevipof-publiee-par-le-cdsp/) is a promising panel, but its secondary-use conditions must be accepted by an eligible user. Its reported 2022 vote is recalled, not verified. Include abstention and blank/null alongside actual election choices. Municipal candidates require a separate local-choice prior; a national party prior does not cover them.
4. `calibrateElectionCell` solves `min_q Σ_i m_i KL(q_i || p_i)` subject to `Σ_i m_i q_ik = Y_gk` and each `q_i` summing to one. It uses exponential tilting of prior odds. It refuses unmatched registered masses or unbalanced official counts. The CLI skips cells with fewer than 30 personas or effective support below 20. Broader/soft constraints should be fit where local support is inadequate; the current CLI reports those cells as uncalibrated instead of inventing local precision.
5. Validate on held-out geographic cells, independent human subgroup proportions and [INSEE observed turnout patterns](https://www.insee.fr/fr/information/6659405), with survey bootstrap/posterior uncertainty. Matching fitted local totals is a consistency check only. Aggregate vote totals cannot identify a persona's choice or persistent cross-election loyalty.

### Implemented historical pilot

`attach_history.py` embeds **observed commune results** for 2022 presidential round 1, 2024 European, 2024 legislative round 1 and 2026 municipal round 1. Exact 2026 COG-code joins cover 1,999 of 2,000 discovery personas in each election. Paris/Lyon/Marseille arrondissements use their parent commune when necessary and are marked separately. The one unmatched row remains missing; historical boundary changes need a dated crosswalk. These records describe places, never a persona's past vote.

`train_history_prior.py` fits an age/sex/broad-occupation/region multinomial model to the openly released [Voter Autrement 2022 respondents](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.18848264). The downloaded CSV has 2,122 records, of which 2,032 have a usable stated intention. The pilot holds out 508 respondents, tunes regularization only on the training portion, then refits and calibrates its intercepts to the **metropolitan national** official 2022 totals on the survey frame. Held-out multiclass log loss is **2.175** versus **2.201** for a survey-prevalence predictor: a small gain of **0.026**. The fitted survey-frame aggregate differs from official category shares by at most **2.5×10⁻¹¹** after calibration. That exact fit is an arithmetic constraint, not predictive validation. The calibrated probabilities vary by persona attributes, but the median strongest category is only **0.268** in this pilot; the available features do not justify manufacturing “die-hard” certainty. These are survey-model estimates, not observed votes. We do **not** force the 2,000-row discovery sample to local totals, where most communes have too few rows to identify individual behavior. The survey measures stated 2022 intentions, and its representativeness and 2027 transport are not established here.

The `/radar` interface takes a dated, sourced event, proposes affected groups using closed tag IDs, and proposes global and group counterfactuals. Both group and global scenarios use the same evaluation path: select at most six personas deterministically across historical-prior choices and ask the LLM for **directional** bloc and abstention reactions to the intervention versus its comparator. Group responses draw from the selected complete-commune frame; global examples draw from the geographically distributed 2,000-row discovery frame for speed. Neither frame represents the whole electorate statistically. The prompt uses the survey prior as weak historical context and local election records as place context. It defaults to “no clear change” without a concrete party-relevant cue and forbids inferring party advantage or private ideology from occupation and synthetic biographies. No 2027 baseline is needed for this relative question. The output is a conditional-on-exposure hypothesis over sampled synthetic rows; displayed counts are not vote shares or population effects. The model supplies no numerical probability shift.

An adjacent human-data check uses [Study 3's randomized US policy-persuasion data](https://doi.org/10.17605/osf.io/8yxvr), with the study's five-item support outcome and treatment-minus-control change by policy and pre-treatment support band. This is a prompt sanity check, **not** a French-election validation. In the current pilot, seven of eight bands have conclusive positive human effects; the direction prompt identified only **3/7**, while the trivial “always toward” rule got **7/7**. The prompt therefore has **not passed even this adjacent benchmark**. We keep its app output labelled as an unvalidated hypothesis and do not use it for an effect estimate. A useful next evaluation needs French event exposure and pre/post intention measured on humans, with negative or null contrasts as well as positive ones, held out from prompt development.

### Minimum-change share calibration pilot

`calibrateElectionShares` accepts normalized observed shares and optional persona representation weights. It minimizes weighted KL change from survey priors under exact aggregate share constraints. The common option multiplier within a cell preserves between-person differences in pairwise log odds, so differing priors do not collapse to a single average. Equal weights are supported as an arithmetic pilot convention; they do not make synthetic rows representative of registered voters. Large shifts remain possible when priors and observed shares disagree, and must be reported rather than hidden by a multiplier penalty.

In the original 2,000-row discovery sample, commune calibration was unsupported: 1,427 communes occur, only 254 have two or more personas, and just one has 30 or more. `scripts/radar/calibrate-discovery-pilot.ts` aggregated official 2022 first-round results to 2026 INSEE regions as an initial diagnostic. Twelve supported metropolitan regions contained 1,947 fitted personas and the largest individual option-probability change was 0.087. This regional pilot is retained for reproducibility; the selected complete-commune frame below now supplies the app's group responses.

`build_population_weights.py` reads [INSEE RP 2022 POP1A](https://www.insee.fr/fr/statistiques/8581810?sommaire=8581933), forming region × sex × age-band adult population cells. It assigns each supported synthetic row its cell's adult population divided by the number of sampled rows in that cell and reports missing-cell coverage. The weighted diagnostic fits the same 12 metropolitan regions and 1,947 personas; its minimum effective row count is about 72 and maximum individual probability change about 0.084. Resident-adult weights do not estimate the 2022 presidential registered electorate, so this remains an exploratory fit.

```sh
npx tsx scripts/radar/calibrate-discovery-pilot.ts
python3 scripts/radar/build_population_weights.py
npx tsx scripts/radar/calibrate-discovery-pilot.ts --weights data/radar/2022-adult-population-weights.jsonl --out data/radar/2022-regional-weighted-pilot.jsonl
```

## Reproduce the import

The files under `data/` are local, git-ignored. Python import requires `pyarrow` and `openpyxl`.

```sh
mkdir -p data/radar/raw
curl -L -o data/radar/raw/general_results.parquet https://data-pipeline-open.s3.sbg.io.cloud.ovh.net/elections/general_results.parquet
curl -L -o data/radar/raw/candidats_results.parquet https://data-pipeline-open.s3.sbg.io.cloud.ovh.net/elections/candidats_results.parquet
python scripts/radar/import_elections.py --general data/radar/raw/general_results.parquet --candidates data/radar/raw/candidats_results.parquet --out-dir data/radar/margins --elections 2022_pres_t1 2022_pres_t2 2024_euro_t1 2024_legi_t1 2026_muni_t1
```

Download the COG commune/department CSV and density XLSX linked above, then run `scripts/radar/build_geography.py` (its `--help` lists arguments). `scripts/radar/build-personas.ts --pages 20` creates a reproducible 2,000-row **discovery sample** spread across Nemotron's full index. The selected complete-commune frame below contains every Nemotron row in its selected places. To inspect narrative tag evidence, run `tsx scripts/radar/export-tag-audit.ts --per-tag 50 --out data/radar/narrative-tag-audit.jsonl`. After a reviewer fills `approved`, `reviewer` and `reviewedAt` on the chosen rows, run `tsx scripts/radar/apply-tag-audit.ts --index data/radar/personas.jsonl --decisions reviewed.jsonl`.

The open survey CSV is distributed by the linked Zenodo record. The historical pilot requires `numpy`, `pandas` and `scikit-learn`; the adjacent response check also requires `openai` and `python-dotenv` plus a configured API key. With those sources downloaded locally:

```sh
python scripts/radar/attach_history.py --index data/radar/personas.jsonl --margins data/radar/margins/2022_pres_t1.jsonl data/radar/margins/2024_euro_t1.jsonl data/radar/margins/2024_legi_t1.jsonl data/radar/margins/2026_muni_t1.jsonl
python scripts/radar/train_history_prior.py --index data/radar/personas.jsonl --survey data/radar/voters.csv --margins data/radar/margins/2022_pres_t1.jsonl --departments data/radar/raw/v_departement_2026.csv
python scripts/radar/backtest_response.py --data data/radar/osf-study3.csv
```

All raw and derived persona data under `data/radar/` are local and git-ignored. The persona manifest records source hashes, join coverage, survey test scores and calibration scope.

## Complete-commune frame and local calibration

`sample_complete_communes.py` selects two whole communes per region × density stratum among places with 5,000–100,000 residents, eight whole sub-1,000-resident communes per region, and all of Paris/Lyon/Marseille including arrondissements. This initial frame covers 12 non-Corsican metropolitan regions; Corsica and overseas places are outside it. It scans the twelve versioned Nemotron Parquet shards and keeps **every row** in each chosen place. The resulting frame has **115,011 rows** in **162 nonempty places** from 170 selected entities; eight very small villages had no Nemotron row. This is a stratified *selection of places*, not a representative population sample or a national vote estimate. The 2022 model and four official local contexts were attached to all 115,011 rows.

`build_population_weights.py --level commune` reads INSEE RP 2022 POP1A adult counts and poststratifies by commune × sex × age band. `calibrate_complete_communes.py` minimizes weighted KL change to each supported commune's official 2022 presidential first-round shares. It requires at least 30 rows, effective support of 20, 95% adult-cell coverage and a maximum single category-probability change of 0.20. **73 communes / 113,975 rows** passed; maximum official-share residual is `9.1e-9`, maximum accepted probability change is **0.175**, and minimum effective support is **47.6**. The other **1,036 rows** keep the nationally calibrated survey prior. Most skipped communes are sparse, and one midsize commune failed the shift threshold. The fitted rows keep a copy of the national prior and explicit calibration scope. Commune adult weights and registered-voter outcome targets have different denominators; the exact fit is a synthetic modeling constraint, not a measured electorate distribution or proof of individual vote accuracy.

```sh
python3 scripts/radar/sample_complete_communes.py
npx tsx scripts/radar/build-personas.ts --raw-input data/radar/complete-communes-raw.jsonl --frame complete_communes --out data/radar/complete-communes-personas.jsonl
python3 scripts/radar/attach_history.py --index data/radar/complete-communes-personas.jsonl --margins data/radar/margins/2022_pres_t1.jsonl data/radar/margins/2024_euro_t1.jsonl data/radar/margins/2024_legi_t1.jsonl data/radar/margins/2026_muni_t1.jsonl
python3 scripts/radar/train_history_prior.py --index data/radar/complete-communes-personas.jsonl --survey data/radar/voters.csv --margins data/radar/margins/2022_pres_t1.jsonl --departments data/radar/raw/v_departement_2026.csv
python3 scripts/radar/build_population_weights.py --index data/radar/complete-communes-personas.jsonl --level commune --out data/radar/2022-commune-adult-weights.jsonl
python3 scripts/radar/calibrate_complete_communes.py --index data/radar/complete-communes-personas.jsonl --weights data/radar/2022-commune-adult-weights.jsonl --out data/radar/complete-communes-calibrated.jsonl
```

Point `RADAR_PERSONA_INDEX` at the calibrated index and `RADAR_GLOBAL_PERSONA_INDEX` at `data/radar/personas.jsonl`. Both indexes and their source manifests remain local under git-ignored `data/radar/`. The selected-commune index is 1.89 GB; any deployment must build or provision the versioned indexes separately. The group API streams the selected-commune index and retains bounded deterministic candidate buckets. The global API streams only the distributed discovery index.

[CEVIPOF ENEF 2024](https://data.sciencespo.fr/dataverse/ENEF2024) publishes seven-wave metadata. Its wave-6 documentation lists 2022 presidential recall, 2024 European and legislative vote responses, demographics and weights, but the respondent CSV/ZIP downloads are restricted (HTTP 403). The user cannot supply access now. [Voter Autrement 2024](https://zenodo.org/records/13898654) is public but self-selected and has limited demographic covariates, so it is not an adequate substitute for independent population calibration or event-response validation. We have not inferred 2024 row vectors from aggregate results alone.

### Remaining evidence gates

- Validate the open survey prior on another human election dataset and check subgroup calibration; the present held-out gain is small.
- Check weight stability and the consequence of matching registered-voter outcome shares with resident-adult synthetic weights. An electorate estimate would require eligibility/registration data.
- Reconcile COG changes for historical elections; publish crosswalk/coverage QA.
- Gather French human pre/post event exposure and intention data, including negative and null contrasts, before reporting numerical subpopulation effects. A dated 2027 intention baseline is additionally required for absolute 2027 forecasts, not for the relative reaction question.

These are input-data and validation requirements, not tasks the Nemotron metadata or official aggregate results can solve by themselves.
